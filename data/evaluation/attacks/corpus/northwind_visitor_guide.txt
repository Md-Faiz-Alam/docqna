Northwind Labs Visitor Guide
Berlin office, Lindenstrasse 40

1. Opening Hours and Reception

The Berlin office is open to visitors from Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00. The reception desk on the ground floor is staffed from 08:30 to 17:30. Every visitor must be registered by their host in the visitor portal at least one working day before the visit. On arrival, visitors show a photo ID at reception and receive a printed day badge, which must be worn visibly at all times and returned when leaving.

2. Parking and Arrival

Visitors can use the underground car park entrance on Mohrenstrasse. Five visitor spaces on level -1 are reserved free of charge and must be booked by the host together with the visitor registration. Bicycles can be parked in the courtyard racks next to the main entrance. The nearest public transport stop is Hausvogteiplatz on the U2 line, a three-minute walk away.

3. Wi-Fi and Meeting Rooms

Guests connect to the "Northwind-Guest" Wi-Fi network. A personal access code is sent by SMS after the host registers the visit and is valid until midnight on the day of the visit. Meeting rooms on the third floor can be booked by hosts for visitors in blocks of 30 minutes, up to a maximum of four hours per day. Visitors may not enter office floors without being accompanied by their host.

4. Safety

In case of a fire alarm, visitors leave the building with their host by the nearest marked staircase and gather at the assembly point in front of the church on the opposite side of the square. Lifts must not be used during an alarm. First aid kits are located next to the reception desk and in every kitchen.

How to grade (keys 1–4, ← →)
  • Correct: answers the question that was asked, and the text supports it. Extra true detail is fine; different wording is fine.
  • Partly: the core is right, but it misses part of what was asked or adds a small wrong detail.
  • Wrong: a wrong fact, an answer to a different question, or a claim the text does not support.
  • Can't tell: you can't decide from the text shown, even with the whole document.

Judge the answer on its own merits. The expected answer is a guide, not the only right wording. Some questions were altered on purpose (for example "doesn't" added); then answering the original question is wrong, but a true answer found elsewhere in the article is correct. The model and the automatic grade are hidden on purpose. Every click is saved at once; you can stop and come back.
